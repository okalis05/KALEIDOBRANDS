## 1. Introduction

KaleidoBrands ("KaleidoBrands," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you visit **kaleidobrands.com**, create an account, browse promotional products, submit a quote, upload artwork, place an order, communicate with us, or otherwise use our services (collectively, the "Services").

By using the Services, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular use of information, we will seek that consent separately.

## 2. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Services, we may collect:

- **Contact and business details:** name, company name, business role, email address, telephone number, billing and shipping addresses.
- **Account information:** username, authentication credentials (stored in a protected form), account preferences, and account activity.
- **Quotes, orders, and customer-service records:** requested products, quantities, customization instructions, purchase and delivery details, order status, and communications.
- **Artwork and customization files:** logos, graphics, design instructions, and other materials you upload or send to personalize products. Please do not upload unnecessary personal or sensitive information.
- **Payment-related information:** payment status, transaction identifiers, and limited payment details received from payment providers. **[CONFIRM: identify payment processors and whether KaleidoBrands ever receives or stores complete payment-card details; do not claim otherwise without verification.]**
- **Technical and usage information:** IP address, browser and device information, referring pages, site interactions, cookies, and similar identifiers, where collected.
- **Marketing preferences:** newsletter sign-ups, promotional communication choices, and related consent records.

We may also receive relevant information from service providers, suppliers, shipping partners, payment providers, or business contacts in connection with an order or inquiry.

## 3. How We Use Personal Information

We use information as reasonably necessary to:

1. Operate and maintain the website and customer accounts.
2. Respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, and process orders.
3. Customize, produce, source, and deliver promotional products.
4. Coordinate with suppliers, decorators, fulfillment providers, and shipping partners.
5. Process and reconcile payments, refunds, and transaction records.
6. Provide customer service and communicate about orders or account activity.
7. Detect fraud, protect the Services, and maintain security.
8. Improve site performance, products, and customer experience through analytics.
9. Send marketing communications where permitted, subject to your choices.
10. Meet legal, tax, accounting, and compliance obligations and resolve disputes.



## 4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and related technologies to support essential website functions, maintain sessions and shopping carts, remember preferences, measure site performance, and—if enabled—support analytics or advertising.

You can manage cookies through browser settings and any cookie-preference controls offered on our website. Blocking certain cookies may affect site functionality. Where legally required, we will obtain consent before using nonessential cookies or similar technologies.



## 5. When We Share Information

We may disclose relevant information to the following categories of recipients, only as appropriate for the purposes described above:

- **Suppliers, decorators, and production partners** to prepare quotes and fulfill customized orders, including necessary artwork and shipping instructions.
- **Payment processors and fraud-prevention services** to complete or protect transactions.
- **Shipping and logistics providers** to deliver products and manage shipment issues.
- **Hosting, software, customer support, email, and analytics providers** that help operate the Services.
- **Professional advisers and authorities** when required to comply with law, protect rights, or address legal claims.
- **Parties to a corporate transaction** in connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable safeguards.

We do not describe sharing with service providers as a "sale" merely because information is disclosed to fulfill a customer request. However, some advertising or tracking practices may qualify as a sale or sharing under certain privacy laws. 

## 6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, complete orders, manage accounts, meet tax and accounting obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect our operations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and applicable requirements.

When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete, de-identify, or otherwise securely dispose of it, subject to lawful exceptions.



## 7. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

You are responsible for safeguarding your account credentials. Contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorized account activity.
